### Why AGPL-3.0?

The AGPL is specifically designed to prevent proprietary use of open-source software in network services:

- **Network copyleft:** Organizations running WulfVault as a SaaS must release their source modifications under AGPL.
- **Attribution preserved:** All modifications must credit the original author.
- **Community benefit:** Improvements made for production deployments flow back to the project.
- **Anti-exploitation:** Prevents commercial "take without giving back" cloud reselling.

This ensures WulfVault remains free and open-source even when used to provide commercial services.
